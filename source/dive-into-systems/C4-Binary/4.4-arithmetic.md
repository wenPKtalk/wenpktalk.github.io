## 4.4. 二进制整数运算

*4.4. Binary Integer Arithmetic*

介绍了[无符号](4.1-bases.html)整数和[有符号](4.3-signed.html)整数的二进制表示之后，我们就可以在算术运算中使用它们了。幸运的是，由于它们的编码方式，无论我们把操作数或结果解释为有符号还是无符号，对算术运算过程来说*都没有区别*。这对硬件设计者来说是个好消息，因为这让他们可以构建一套硬件部件，同时用于无符号和有符号运算。[硬件一章](../C5-Arch/5.4-circuits.html)会更详细地介绍执行算术运算的电路。

> Having presented binary representations for [unsigned](4.1-bases.html) and [signed](4.3-signed.html) integers, we’re ready to use them in arithmetic operations. Fortunately, due to their encoding, it *does not matter* to the arithmetic procedures whether we choose to interpret the operands or result as signed or unsigned. This observation is great news for hardware designers because it allows them to build one set of hardware components that can be shared for both unsigned and signed operations. The [hardware chapter](../C5-Arch/5.4-circuits.html) describes the circuitry for performing arithmetic in more detail.

幸运的是，你在小学学过的用纸笔对十进制数做算术的算法，同样适用于二进制数。尽管硬件计算它们的方式可能不完全相同，但你至少应该能看懂这些计算。

> Luckily, the same pencil-and-paper algorithms you learned in grade school for performing arithmetic on decimal numbers also work for binary numbers. Though the hardware might not compute them in exactly the same way, you should at least be able to make sense of the calculations.
